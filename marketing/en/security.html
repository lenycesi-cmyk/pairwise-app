Security & privacy

Mechanisms, not adjectives.

"Bank-grade security", "military-grade encryption": everyone writes it, nobody can check it. Here is what PairWise actually does with your data, who it protects you from — and what it does not do yet.

Where it starts

No trackers. No ads. Nothing to sell.

A budget tells where you live, what you eat, when you travel and how much you earn. Exactly the kind of data an ad network loves. PairWise sends it to no one, because it carries nothing that could.

  • No analytics tooling — no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Meta Pixel.
  • No ad network, and no business model that would depend on one.
  • No selling, no commercial sharing of your data, in any form.
  • Your insights are computed on your device — the "For you" suggestions never reach a server.
Check it yourself, without taking our word for it

Open PairWise, press F12, go to the "Network" tab and reload. You will see exactly what the app contacts. It is the one promise on this page you can verify in thirty seconds — which is why we put it first.

Transparency

What leaves your device, and what never does

An app that shows live prices and exchange rates necessarily makes network calls. Claiming otherwise would fall apart on first inspection. Here they all are.

The part that matters

Who we protect you from

Protection means nothing until you name the threat. Here are four, each with the mechanism behind it.

From other users Access to a space is checked on the server, on every read, against the member list. A malformed space is denied to everyone rather than left open by default. Joining a couple goes through a server function only: nobody can add themselves.
From your partner An expense or an account can be marked private: it stays invisible to the other, including in totals and reports. Sharing your finances should never mean showing everything — it is the privacy nobody talks about, and often the one that matters most.
From a stolen invite code A space's code expires after 7 days and closes the moment your partner has used it. Outside that window no code works. Codes come from a cryptographic generator, never from ordinary programming randomness.
From a bank-side leak Bank access tokens live in a space the app cannot read — only the server can. Even someone taking over your browser could not extract them.
What really sets us apart

Our access rules are tested before every release

A security rule can deploy with no error at all and still protect nothing: the mistakes only show at evaluation, once your data is behind them. It is a classic trap, and it has cost many applications dearly.

  • Our rules are run against the real security engine on every change, with fake accounts trying to reach what isn't theirs.
  • That test suite is blocking: if a test fails, the release does not happen. It is not a good intention, it is a lock.
  • One test deliberately keeps a broken rule as a sentinel, to confirm our tests can still detect a genuine hole.

We are not asking you to trust us: we are telling you what is automated, because a process holds where individual vigilance eventually slips.

Honesty

What PairWise does not do

This is the most important section on the page. A list of guarantees with no stated limits means nothing — and you can only judge the first by reading the second.

  • No end-to-end encryption. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our host, but our servers can technically read it. That is what makes the nightly net-worth snapshot and bank syncing possible. We will not pretend otherwise.
  • Leaving does not erase everything. If you close your account while your partner keeps going, the couple's history stays: it belongs to them too. It is all erased when the last member leaves.
  • We are not a bank and never hold your money. PairWise reads balances; it moves no funds.
  • No external security audit has been carried out yet. The day there is one, it will be written here, with its date and scope.
Your control

Leave whenever you want, with everything

A finance app that holds its users through their data does not believe in itself.

  • Full export in one click — Settings → My data. A readable file you keep wherever you want, and can import back.
  • Delete means delete. Removing a transaction also removes the receipt photo; it is no longer reachable, even through a link obtained earlier.
  • Closing your account revokes bank access and erases your data. When the last member leaves, receipts and photos are purged along with the rest.
Frequently asked

What people ask us most

Does PairWise sell my data?+

No. The app ships no tracker, no analytics tool and no ad network — so there is nothing to sell, and nothing to switch off. You can verify it yourself in your browser's network tab.

Is my data end-to-end encrypted?+

No, and we would rather write it plainly. It is encrypted in transit and at rest by our host, but our servers can technically read it: that is what lets us record your net worth every evening and sync your bank accounts.

Can my partner see everything I record?+

No. An expense or an account can be marked private: it stays invisible to the other member, including in totals and reports.

What happens to my receipt when I delete a transaction?+

The image is deleted from storage along with the transaction. It stops being reachable, including for someone who saved its sharing link earlier.

What happens if I delete my account?+

Your account and credentials go immediately. The couple's history stays as long as your partner keeps using PairWise — it belongs to them too. When the last member closes their account, everything is deleted: transactions, receipts, photos and bank connections.

Where is my data hosted?+

On Google Cloud, the infrastructure behind Firebase. Our server-side processing runs in the European region europe-west1 (Belgium).
